# 01 — Executive Vision

## The one-sentence vision

Avocado is the **instructional operating system** for an elementary school — a single platform that turns assessment data into a specific, differentiated, standards-aligned plan for what every teacher should teach tomorrow, and gives every leader the visibility to support it.

## The problem we solve

In a large district like Miami-Dade, the data exists — FAST PM1/2/3, i-Ready, district topic and interim assessments, exit tickets — but it lives in a dozen disconnected systems and static PDFs. Teachers and coaches spend hours *assembling* data instead of *acting* on it. The gap between "we have the data" and "here is the small-group lesson for these five students on this standard tomorrow morning" is where student growth is lost.

Avocado closes that gap. It ingests the data schools already generate, identifies the standards and students that need attention, and produces ready-to-teach, differentiated, printable instruction — automatically.

## Why now

- **Florida B.E.S.T. standards** and the **FAST** progress-monitoring model create three high-stakes data checkpoints a year, rewarding schools that can respond quickly between them.
- **MTSS/RTI** is a district expectation, but the paperwork and grouping logic is manual and slow.
- **AI has crossed the threshold** where it can reliably generate standards-aligned, grade-appropriate instructional materials and coach a teacher through a data conversation.
- School grade is decided at the margins — the "bubble" students and the lowest-25% growth. A tool that surfaces exactly those students and exactly what they need is directly tied to the accountability outcome that matters most to a principal.

## What success looks like

| Stakeholder | Success means |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Teacher** | Walks in Monday with groups formed, a 7-day DI plan, and printable packets already generated from Friday's exit ticket. Saves 3–5 hours of planning per week. |
| **Coach** | PLC agendas, data discussions, and root-cause analyses are auto-drafted. Coaching time shifts from data-wrangling to actual coaching. |
| **Principal / AP** | Opens one dashboard and instantly sees school health, who is at risk, which standards are failing school-wide, and whether interventions are working. |
| **District** | Comparable, real-time visibility across schools; early-warning on schools trending away from an A. |
| **Student** | Gets instruction targeted to their actual gaps, faster, with growth they and their family can see. |

## The north-star metric

**Time-to-instruction:** the elapsed time from an assessment being scored to a teacher having a differentiated, standards-aligned lesson in hand for the right students. Today that is measured in days-to-weeks (or never). Avocado's target is **minutes**.

Secondary metrics: percentage of tested standards with an active DI plan; lowest-25% growth trend; teacher weekly active use; reduction in planning hours.

## Product principles

1. **Answer the question, don't just show the data.** Every screen ends in a recommended action, not a chart to interpret.
2. **Automate the assembly, keep the judgment human.** Avocado does the grouping, drafting, and formatting. Educators approve and adapt.
3. **Standards are the spine.** Every student, assessment, group, and lesson connects back to a Florida B.E.S.T. standard.
4. **Printable and offline-friendly.** Elementary classrooms run on paper and spotty Wi-Fi. Every packet exports to PDF.
5. **Fast, clean, and teacher-first.** If a teacher needs training to use it, we designed it wrong.
6. **Trustworthy by construction.** Compliance and human-in-the-loop are features, not friction.

## The business trajectory

Avocado is designed first for a single M-DCPS elementary school (the "Avocado Elementary" pilot), then hardened for a set of Title I feeder schools, then the district, then licensable to any Florida district, and ultimately configurable for any U.S. state's standards and accountability model. The architecture (see [06](06-system-architecture.md)) treats "standards framework" and "accountability model" as configuration, not hard-coded assumptions, precisely so this trajectory is possible.
